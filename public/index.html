Terms of Recollection

— being the clauses under which this engine agrees to remember things —
1

The Extraction Clause. Any text submitted shall be broken into atomic, standalone facts, each classified as a Preference, Task, or Observation, entirely by a model running on this machine.

2

The Embedding Clause. Every fact shall be converted into a 768-dimensional vector and stored in Postgres via pgvector, alongside a full-text index, for future retrieval.

3

The Conflict Clause. Before a new fact is filed, similar existing facts (cosine similarity > 0.55) are checked by the model for contradiction. A confirmed contradiction renders the old fact outdated, not deleted.

4

The Expiry Clause. Facts deemed time-sensitive by the extraction model shall self-expire after 7 days.

5

The Locality Clause. No fact, embedding, or query shall ever leave this machine. There is no API key because there is no third party.
